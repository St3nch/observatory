   Mentions/search, target and multi-target metrics, top pages/domains/brands/categories,
   historical/timeseries/new-and-lost forms, AI Keyword Data, LLM Responses, and provider
   products named LLM Scraper. Correct this list against current official documentation.
2. For each materially distinct contract, record analytical questions answered, indexed
   versus active-measurement meaning, update cadence/data period, model/platform/location/
   language context, useful returned fields and relationships, provider-native identifiers,
   request options/enrichments, pagination or continuation, total/returned counts, known
   truncation/sampling, response-size implications, and current cost.
3. Map overlap and disagreement value against accepted Keyword Overview and Google Organic
   testimony. Do not collapse unlike demand, SERP AIO, indexed mention, active answer, or
   citation facts.
4. Determine which contracts fit one bounded exchange honestly and which require
   task/poll, continuation, multiple pages, or response-derived follow-up provenance not yet
   accepted by Observatory.
5. Assess retention, privacy/personal-data, provider-terms, and API-redistribution risks,
   especially for answer text, prompts, snippets, citations, and source/page material.
6. Identify candidate semantic identities and occurrence relationships without writing a
   recipe: requested target versus returned identity; mention/citation/source/page/domain/
   brand; model/platform; period/time; duplicates; ordering; parent/child attachment; and
   absence/completeness boundaries.

   material contract branch; give exact purpose, request options, expected learning,
   maximum call count, and conservative worst-case cost. A proposal is not authorization.
8. Recommend one exact first adapter contract or recommend stopping. Explain why it creates
   useful reusable learning for later AI surfaces without inventing a premature universal
   AI abstraction.
